# 🚩 Master Business Problems & Integrated Solutions (Nasim’s Model)

### 1. Inventory & Dead Stock (Capital Blockage)
* **Problem:** 127 Components and 15 Clothing items are stagnant.
* **Solution (Psychological Bundling):** Bundle these as "Free Gifts" with high-selling bikes. Embed clothing costs into bike prices to clear stock and boost satisfaction.

### 2. Revenue vs. Profit Efficiency (The Profitability Trap)
* **Problem:** High-revenue series (Road-250) have lower profit margins (Volume Drivers).
* **Solution (Cross-Category Bundling):** Package low-margin bikes with high-margin accessories to offset margins and boost net profit.

### 3. Seasonal Sales & Operational Load (The December Peak)
* **Problem:** Slow Jan–May followed by an extreme December peak creates cash-flow gaps.
* **Solution (Monthly Maintenance & Pre-Booking):** Launch **Monthly Maintenance Campaigns** during slow months and introduce a Pre-booking model in March–April.
